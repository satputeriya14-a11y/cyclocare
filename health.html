🌷 Health Information

Understanding your menstrual cycle can help you keep track of changes in your body and maintain healthy habits.

🩸 Understanding the Menstrual Cycle

The menstrual cycle is a natural process involving hormonal changes in the body. The cycle includes menstruation and other phases that prepare the body for a possible pregnancy.

Cycle length can vary from person to person and may also change from one cycle to another.

📅 About Irregular Cycles

A period may sometimes arrive earlier or later than expected. Changes in routine, stress, sleep, illness, exercise and other factors can affect the menstrual cycle.

Tracking your periods regularly can help you notice changes in your usual pattern.

⚕️ When to Seek Professional Advice

If you notice persistent or unusual changes in your menstrual cycle, severe discomfort, or other symptoms that concern you, consider discussing them with a qualified healthcare professional.

CycloCare provides tracking and general wellness information. It does not diagnose medical conditions.

🧼 Period Hygiene

  • Change menstrual products regularly.
  • Keep the genital area clean and dry.
  • Wash your hands before and after changing products.
  • Dispose of used menstrual products properly.
  • Stay hydrated and maintain regular meals.

🌿 General Wellness Tips

  • Get adequate sleep and rest.
  • Stay physically active according to your comfort.
  • Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Eat a balanced and varied diet.
  • Keep track of symptoms and cycle changes.
